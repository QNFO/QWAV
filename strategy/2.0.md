Quantum computing and artificial intelligence share a hidden assumption: that space is continuous ($\mathbb{R}^n$, Archimedean geometry). This assumption is wrong for the problems these fields actually solve -- problems of hierarchy, scale, branching structure, and discrete classification.

QWAV replaces Archimedean (continuous) geometry with ultrametric (tree-based, $p$-adic) geometry. The Bruhat-Tits tree -- the geometric object at the heart of $p$-adic number theory -- provides:

- **Passive fault tolerance in quantum computing:** The strong triangle inequality ($d(x,z) \leq \max\{d(x,y), d(y,z)\}$) geometrically suppresses errors. No active quantum error correction needed. Operation at $4\,\text{K}$.
- **Glass-box explainability in AI:** Decision paths through the tree are traceable by construction. The Syntactic Token Calculus verification protocol achieves $100\%$ detection rate with zero false positives -- properties that are mathematically impossible in continuous embedding spaces.

**One sentence:** Quantum computing is stalled not because of bad engineering but because of a bad mathematical assumption -- that space is continuous. Ultrametric geometry corrects that assumption.

## 3. VISION LAYERS -- Personal / Program / Public

The program operates at three distinct layers with different audiences, constraints, and success criteria. These layers must be coherent but not identical. What Rowan wants privately is not what QWAV says publicly -- and that's correct.

### 3.1 Personal Vision (Rowan Brad Quni-Gudzinas)

**What Rowan wants:**
- **Intellectual legacy:** To be recognized as the person who corrected a foundational mathematical error in quantum computing and AI -- the geometry assumption. Not fame. Not wealth. Recognition among those who understand the work.
- **Autonomy:** Freedom from institutional gatekeeping, credential requirements, funding cycles, and academic politics. The work proceeds at its own pace, on its own terms.
- **The introvert path:** A sustainable, energizing workflow that does not require live performance, networking, or self-promotion. Written communication. Deep work. No burnout.
- **Financial sustainability:** Not wealth, but enough to continue the work without distraction. Funding is a means, not an end.
- **Intellectual satisfaction:** The work itself is rewarding. The program exists because the ideas are compelling and the computational results are beautiful -- not because there's a business model.

**What Rowan does NOT want:**
- A startup (team, VCs, board meetings, growth metrics)
- Academic prestige (PhD, tenure, journal publications, conference keynotes)
- Fame or public attention (media, podcasts, "thought leadership")
- To convince everyone -- only the right people

**Personal success metric:** A single inbound contact from a qualified researcher who read the work, understood it, and was moved to engage. That's the signal that the ideas have escaped the founder's own mind and landed in someone else's. Everything else -- publications, applications, social posts -- is activity, not outcome.

### 3.2 Program Vision (QWAV Internal)

**What QWAV achieves as a program:**
- **A self-sustaining evidence engine:** Computational validation produces reproducible, citable results. Each spinoff strengthens the core thesis. The 673-release corpus provides intellectual depth. The program can continue indefinitely at near-zero cost.
- **A spinoff machine:** Ideas are formalized as spinoff specifications → delegated to Projects agent → executed → published as interactive artifacts + DOIs → cross-referenced back into the program. This is a repeatable, scalable knowledge-production pipeline.
- **A credibility architecture:** The program does not ask to be believed. It asks to be tested. Every publication includes falsifiable predictions. Every codebase is open-source. Every method is documented. Credibility is earned through reproducibility, not credentials.
- **Inbound-only engagement:** The program does not chase. It publishes and waits. If the work is compelling, the right people will find it. If they don't, the work continues anyway.

**Program success metrics:**
- **Evidence depth:** More computational results, broader validation, more spinoffs
- **Publication permanence:** Everything has a DOI. Nothing can be taken down or memory-holed.
- **Inbound signal:** At least one substantive contact per 6-month cycle
- **Application conversion:** At least one acceptance from 6 pending applications
- **Self-sustainability:** The program can operate indefinitely without external funding

### 3.3 Public Vision (What the World Sees)

**What QWAV says to the world:**
- **The thesis:** "Quantum computing is stalled because of a bad mathematical assumption. We fixed it. Here's the evidence."
- **The evidence:** Computational validation showing zero logical errors at depth 7, $48\times$ error reduction, glass-box AI that outperforms transformers. Falsifiable predictions. Open-source code.
- **The posture:** Confident but not arrogant. Evidence-first. "Don't believe us -- run the simulation yourself." No credential claims. No institutional name-dropping. The work speaks.
- **The aesthetic:** Clean, mathematical, evidence-driven. Not corporate. Not academic. Independent. The visual identity (when developed) should reflect geometric elegance -- trees, ultrametric structure, $p$-adic patterns.

**Public success metric:** A reader -- not personally known to the founder -- who encounters QWAV content, engages with an interactive artifact, reads the paper, and concludes: "This is real. This is important." That reader becomes an advocate, not because they were asked to, but because the work earned it.

**What the public does NOT see:**
- The personal vision (Rowan's private motivations)
- The application pipeline (who was applied to, what was rejected)
- The strategy documents (these are internal)
- The piecemeal history (the public sees the coherent program, not the 673-release raw material)
- Any hint of desperation, insecurity, or supplication
