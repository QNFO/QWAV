audience and purpose while reinforcing the others. This document defines the
boundaries, messaging, and relationship between them.

## 1. QNFO -- The Institution

### Identity
**Scientific research incubator.** The organizational home for all non-commercial
open-science work. Provides institutional credibility, legal structure, and a
stable reference point for the public, collaborators, and funders.

### Key Messages
- "Advancing scientific understanding for the collective benefit of all"
- Research incubator — advancing scientific understanding
- All research open-access, all code open-source, all governance public
- Multiple initiatives, one mission

### Audiences
- **Academic:** Researchers evaluating the work's credibility
- **Institutional:** Grant committees, fellowship reviewers
- **Public:** Anyone encountering QNFO through publications or media
- **Legal:** License enforcement, IP protection

### Tone
Institutional, mission-driven, forward-looking. Confident but not boastful.
Emphasizes structure, governance, and long-term vision.

### Primary Touchpoints
| Touchpoint | URL | Purpose |
|:-----------|:----|:--------|
| Org Profile | [github.com/QNFO](https://github.com/QNFO) | First discovery |
| Org README | QNFO/.github | Mission, portfolio, values |
| Website | [qnfo.org](https://qnfo.org) | Public-facing home |
| License | [github.com/QNFO/license](https://github.com/QNFO/license) | Legal governance |

### What NOT to Say
- ❌ "QNFO is QWAV" -- QWAV is one initiative, not the whole org
- ❌ "We are a startup" -- QNFO is an open-science research incubator
- ❌ Booster language ("revolutionary," "breakthrough") -- let evidence speak

## 2. QWAV -- The Flagship

### Identity
**Ultrametric quantum computing & glass-box AI research program.** The most
active and visible QNFO initiative. QWAV is where the computational physics
happens. It is a subsidiary of QNFO, not synonymous with it.

### Key Messages
- "Passive fault tolerance. Glass-box AI. One mathematical correction."
- Bruhat-Tits tree geometry replaces Archimedean geometry
- Zero logical errors at depth 7; 48× error reduction
- All findings published open-access with registered DOIs
- **"QWAV is the first-mover in ultrametric quantum computing architecture"** — 5 DOI-registered publications. Open standards. Open validation. Solo researcher. ([Wiki: Manufacturing Blueprint](https://github.com/QNFO/QWAV/wiki/Manufacturing-Blueprint))
- **4K operation, not mK** — 400× warmer, commodity cooling, 20,000× more thermal headroom
- **Tree topology works on ALL platforms** — software reconfiguration or mask change, not new physics

### Audiences
- **Technical:** Quantum computing researchers, physicists, AI/ML engineers
- **Industry:** Neutral-atom hardware companies (PASQAL, QuEra, Atom, Infleqtion); superconducting (IBM, Google); trapped ion (IonQ, Quantinuum); silicon spin (Intel, Diraq); foundries (TSMC, GlobalFoundries)
- **Fellowship:** Emergent Ventures, Foresight Institute, etc.
- **Funding/Grants:** First-mover in ultrametric quantum architecture — 5 DOI-registered publications establishing priority. Solo researcher. Open architecture initiative. Not a startup seeking exit.

### Tone
Technical, evidence-driven, specific. Cites DOIs. Shows interactive demos.
Welcomes scrutiny. Avoids marketing language -- the math speaks.

### Primary Touchpoints
| Touchpoint | URL | Purpose |
|:-----------|:----|:--------|
| QWAV README | [github.com/QNFO/QWAV](https://github.com/QNFO/QWAV) | Technical overview |
| Technical Hub | [qnfo.github.io/QWAV](https://qnfo.github.io/QWAV/) | Interactive demos |
| Artifacts | A1-A5 + K1 repos | Live demos |
| Publications | Zenodo community | Papers with DOIs |

### What NOT to Say
- ❌ "QWAV is QNFO" -- QWAV is the flagship, not the institution
- ❌ "We've proven..." -- We've demonstrated computational validation; proof requires replication
- ❌ "The only solution..." -- Open to alternative approaches

## 4. Interlinking Rules

### When to Reference QNFO
- In any institutional context: grants, fellowships, legal documents
- When describing the organizational structure
- On the QWAV README as the parent organization
- In publication author affiliations

### When to Reference QWAV
- In any technical context about ultrametric quantum computing
- When linking to interactive demos or research papers
- On the QNFO README as the flagship initiative

### When to Reference Rowan
- In any personal context: LinkedIn, bio, speaking engagements
- In publication author lists and ORCID records
- On personal website or portfolio

### Cross-Referencing Format
```
QNFO → mentions QWAV as "flagship research initiative"
QWAV → mentions QNFO as "parent research incubator"
QWAV → authored by "Rowan Brad Quni-Gudzinas"
Rowan → mentions QNFO as "institutional research identity"
Rowan → links to QWAV as "primary research program"
Rowan → maintains rwnq8 as "personal research portfolio"
```
