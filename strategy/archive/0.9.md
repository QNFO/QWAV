## 3. CRITICAL RESEARCH QUESTION -- ANSWER THIS FIRST

**The QWAV thread raised a key question:** Why Bruhat-Tits trees specifically instead of more general ultrametric/cophenetic architecture?

| Approach | Pros | Cons |
|:---------|:-----|:-----|
| **Bruhat-Tits trees** ($\mathcal{T}_p$, fixed $p$) | Rich algebraic structure (group action, Langlands connection). Same tree as quantum side -- strongest form of common thesis. | Fixed branching factor $p+1$. Not data-adaptive. May be over-constrained for pure AI applications. |
| **Cophenetic/dendrogram trees** (learned from data) | Data-adaptive. Any hierarchical clustering produces an ultrametric tree. More flexible for AI. Already proposed in UCLM spec. | Less algebraic structure. Weaker connection to quantum side (different tree for AI vs. quantum). |
| **Hybrid** (Bruhat-Tits backbone + cophenetic refinement) | Combines algebraic rigor with data adaptivity. | Complexity. Two formalisms to reconcile. |

**Your first deliverable:** A clear recommendation on this question with rationale grounded in the prior work. This determines the entire specification's mathematical foundation.
