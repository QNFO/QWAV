## Quick Reference

**QWAV is the first-mover in ultrametric quantum computing architecture** — 5 DOI-registered publications establishing priority in a field where the standard approach has not delivered a commercially useful quantum computation.

The standard approach (active QEC on Euclidean lattices, surface codes) faces a thermodynamic wall — a 20,000× cooling gap that is Carnot-limited. `[EST]` The ultrametric alternative (Bruhat-Tits trees, passive geometric fault tolerance) achieves 75× better thresholds computationally. QWAV is one person. The approach is different.

"QWAV is the first-mover in ultrametric quantum computing — an open architecture initiative. We publish open standards, coordinate research, and validate implementations. We don't manufacture. We don't license IP. We don't compete with hardware companies. We propose the standard. They build."
