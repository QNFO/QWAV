## 3. QWAV Fit Assessment for FQXi Essay Contests

**Strengths:**
- FQXi values "expansive, risky topics" and foundational questions -- QWAV's thesis (ultrametric geometry as universal organizing principle) is exactly this kind of foundational work
- Essays are 2,500-25,000 words -- QWAV has extensive material to draw from (~40 publications)
- Open to unaffiliated researchers -- no academic affiliation required
- Public + expert judging -- good for visibility regardless of outcome

**Weaknesses:**
- Topic-dependent -- if 2026 topic is narrowly focused (e.g., consciousness, AI ethics), QWAV fit may be poor
- Competitive -- 100+ entries typically
- Single-author essays may face implicit bias vs. institutional authors

**Optimal FQXi topic for QWAV:** "Foundations of quantum computation" or "Mathematical structures underlying physical law" -- anything touching p-adic physics, ultrametric hierarchies, or non-standard geometries.
