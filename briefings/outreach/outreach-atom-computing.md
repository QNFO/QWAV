My work explores whether tree-based (ultrametric) geometry can reduce quantum errors without active error correction. Computational validation suggests this is possible under certain conditions.

I have published this work open-access and built several interactive demonstrations, including one that shows how errors injected at leaf atoms get geometrically contained as they propagate upward through the tree structure:

https://qnfo.github.io/hardware-pathway/

All evidence and artifacts at:
https://qnfo.github.io/QWAV/

I am an independent researcher looking for feedback from groups with experimental expertise. If you have comments on the feasibility of this geometric approach, I would welcome them.
