## 1. Program Overview

The **Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR)** program was reauthorized by the *Small Business Innovation and Economic Security Act* (S. 3971), signed into law **April 13, 2026**. This is current and active.

**Three-phase structure:**
| Phase | Purpose | Funding | Duration |
|:------|:--------|:--------|:---------|
| Phase I | Feasibility / proof-of-concept | Up to ~$150K-$275K (agency-dependent) | 6-12 months |
| Phase II | Prototype / development | Up to ~$1M-$1.6M | 2 years |
| Phase III | Commercialization | No SBIR funds -- private/agency follow-on | -- |

## 6. QWAV's Competitive Position

**Strengths for SBIR:**
- Novel error correction paradigm (ultrametric/tree-based) -- differentiated from surface codes
- Mathematical rigor (Bruhat-Tits trees, p-adic analysis) -- well-suited for feasibility demonstration
- Cross-cutting: quantum computing + AI implications
- ~40 publications/whitepapers demonstrate sustained research capability

**Weaknesses:**
- No entity exists (prerequisite)
- No lab/experimental validation yet
- Solo founder -- reviewers may question team depth
- No prior government funding track record

**Mitigation:** Phase I is specifically for feasibility -- solo founder with novel theory fits. Partner with university lab for Phase II if needed.
